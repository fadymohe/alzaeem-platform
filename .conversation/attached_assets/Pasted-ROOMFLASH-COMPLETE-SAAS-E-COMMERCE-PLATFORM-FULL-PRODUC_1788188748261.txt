==================================================
1. CORE BUSINESS CONCEPT
==================================================

RoomFlash is a SaaS platform that allows merchants to create their own online stores.

A merchant signs up.

The merchant creates a store.

RoomFlash automatically creates a storefront under a RoomFlash subdomain.

Example:

storename.roomflash.com

The merchant can:
- Add products
- Add product images
- Set prices
- Set inventory
- Set product weight
- Receive orders
- Manage customers
- Connect shipping providers
- Create shipments
- Track shipments
- View analytics
- Run marketing campaigns
- Manage subscriptions

Paid merchants can connect their own custom domain.

Example:

www.mystore.com
